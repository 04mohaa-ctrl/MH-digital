# Product Requirements Document: MH Digital MVP

## Overview

### Product Name

**MH Digital**

### Tagline

**We build modern websites that work as hard as you do, build trust, and help your business attract more customers.**

### Problem Statement

Many local small businesses either do not have a website or rely on outdated websites and poorly maintained social media pages. This creates a weak first impression, makes information difficult to find, and limits their ability to attract new customers.

### MVP Goal

Build a strong portfolio, acquire the first customers, and establish a scalable workflow for creating modern websites.

### Target Launch

Within one month, MH Digital's own website and portfolio should be completed.

---

# Target Users

## Primary User Profile

### Who

Owners of local small businesses such as:

- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Hair salons
- Auto repair shops
- Other local businesses

### Typical Customer

- Between 30 and 60 years old
- Limited technical knowledge
- Wants to grow the business
- Has little time to manage technology
- Focuses on running the business rather than maintaining websites

### Current Solution

- No website at all
- Old or poorly functioning website
- Facebook page
- Google Maps profile

### Why They'll Switch

- Modern design
- Professional appearance
- Personal communication
- Affordable pricing
- Tailored solutions for their business

# Quality Standards

## Code Quality

### Principles

- Reusable components over duplicated code
- Simplicity over unnecessary complexity
- Readability over cleverness
- Long-term maintainability over short-term speed

### Standards

- No duplicated code when reusable components are possible
- Consistent naming conventions
- Clear folder structure
- AI-generated code must always be reviewed before deployment
- Production code should prioritize maintainability

---

## Design Quality

### Principles

- Premium look and feel
- Mobile-first experience
- Fast loading speeds
- Consistent typography and spacing
- Clear calls-to-action

### We Do Not Accept

- Generic AI-looking websites
- Poor mobile experiences
- Slow loading pages
- Inconsistent design systems
- Placeholder content in production
- Broken links or unfinished sections

---

# Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| Few customers | High | Daily outreach and portfolio building |
| Too many projects | Medium | Limit active projects to 3–5 |
| Lack of time | High | University studies remain top priority |
| Inconsistent quality | High | Standardized templates and workflows |
| Scope creep | Medium | Focus only on websites in V1 |
| Burnout | Medium | Keep MH Digital as a side project initially |
| Difficulty finding clients | High | Build demos and maintain continuous outreach |
