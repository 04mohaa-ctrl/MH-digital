# Outreach Process — MH Digital

## Goal
Acquire first customers through daily, consistent outreach to local businesses with weak or no web presence.

## Daily Routine (15–30 min)
1. Identify 3–5 local businesses (restaurants, cafés, salons, auto shops)
2. Note their current digital presence (no site, outdated site, Facebook only)
3. Send one personalized email or make one phone call
4. Log contact in a simple spreadsheet (name, business, date, status)

## First Contact Script (Email)
**Subject:** Quick idea for [Business Name]'s online presence

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Business Name] — [specific observation about their current web/social presence].

I build modern, mobile-friendly websites for local businesses in [area]. I'd love to show you a quick demo of what yours could look like — no obligation.

Would you be open to a 10-minute call this week?

Best,
[Your name]
MH Digital

## Follow-Up
- Day 3: Short follow-up if no reply
- Day 7: Final follow-up with portfolio link
- Mark as "cold" and revisit in 30 days

## Demo Presentation
1. Show MH Digital portfolio page (`/portfolio`)
2. Open relevant industry demo (`/demos/restaurant`, etc.)
3. Walk through mobile view
4. Explain process and timeline (2–4 weeks)

## Proposal Checklist
- [ ] Scope defined (pages, features)
- [ ] Price agreed
- [ ] Timeline set
- [ ] Content collection plan
- [ ] Deposit/terms (if applicable)
