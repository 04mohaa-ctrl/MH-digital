# Product Requirements

## Product Overview
- **Name:** MH Digital
- **Tagline:** We build modern websites that work as hard as you do, build trust, and help your business attract more customers.
- **MVP Goal:** Build a strong portfolio, acquire the first customers, and establish a scalable workflow for creating modern websites.
- **Target Launch:** Within one month — MH Digital's own website and portfolio completed.

## Primary User Story
**Ahmed — Restaurant Owner** (Technical Level: Low)

Ahmed owns a local restaurant. He wants to attract more customers and build a professional image, but information about his business is scattered across Facebook and Google Maps. He doesn't have time or knowledge to build a website himself. He needs a modern, mobile-friendly site that makes his menu, hours, and contact info easy to find — delivered by someone who communicates personally and offers affordable, tailored solutions.

## Must-Have Features (MVP)

### 1. MH Digital Website
- **Purpose:** Present the business and establish trust
- **Success criteria:** Contact information available, services clearly presented, works on desktop and mobile
- **Key pages:** Home (hero, services overview, portfolio preview, process, contact CTA), Services, Portfolio, Contact
- **Priority:** Critical

### 2. Portfolio and Case Studies
- **Purpose:** Showcase quality and previous work
- **Success criteria:** At least three demo websites with clear project descriptions
- **Key elements:** Project cards, case studies, before/after examples, call-to-action
- **Priority:** Critical

### 3. Contact Channels
- **Purpose:** Make it easy for customers to get in touch
- **Success criteria:** Working contact form, visible email address, visible phone number
- **Priority:** Critical

### 4. Proposal and Delivery Process
- **Purpose:** Provide a clear and professional customer experience
- **Success criteria:** Documented steps — initial contact → demo presentation → proposal → development → delivery
- **Priority:** Critical

## Success Metrics

### Short-Term (Month 1)
- Launch the MH Digital website
- Complete at least three demo websites
- Define a standardized workflow
- Begin daily outreach

### Medium-Term (Month 3)
- Acquire the first five clients
- Improve the sales process
- Begin building a reusable component library

### Long-Term
- Consistently acquire 3–5 new clients per month
- Generate stable side income
- Create a semi-automated workflow
- Build the foundation for a full-time business

## UI/UX Requirements

### Design Feel
Modern, premium, professional, fast, simple

### Inspiration
Stripe, Vercel, Linear, Apple, Framer

### Design Principles
- **Mobile first** — every experience works perfectly on mobile
- **Premium experience** — fewer sections, higher quality
- **Performance first** — fast websites with excellent Lighthouse scores
- **Clear CTAs** — the next step should always be obvious
- **Simplicity** — avoid unnecessary complexity

### Key Pages

| Page | Purpose | Key Elements |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Home | Present MH Digital, build trust | Hero, services overview, portfolio preview, process, contact CTA |
| Portfolio | Show previous work | Project cards, case studies, before/after, CTA |
| Services | Explain the offer | Services overview, what's included, process, FAQ, contact CTA |
| Contact | Generate leads | Contact form, email, phone number |

## Technical Requirements
- **Platform:** Web, responsive, mobile-first
- **Performance:** Load time under 3 seconds, Lighthouse 90+, SEO-friendly structure, smooth animations
- **Security:** HTTPS, form protection, basic security practices
- **Scalability:** Reusable components, AI-assisted workflows, standardized templates
- **Browsers:** Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge
- **Devices:** Mobile, tablet, desktop

## Constraints
- **Budget:** Low — only invest in tools with high ROI (~$20–30/month total)
- **Timeline:** One month for MVP
- **Technical preferences:** Code + AI, professional but pragmatic stack, reusability over complexity
- **Main constraint:** University studies remain the highest priority
